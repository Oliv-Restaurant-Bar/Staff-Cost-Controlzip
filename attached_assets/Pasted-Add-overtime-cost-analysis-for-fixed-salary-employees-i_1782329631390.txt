Add overtime cost analysis for fixed-salary employees in actual hours and personnel cost reporting.

Goal:
When fixed-salary employees work overtime, show the overtime as separate additional personnel cost.

Definition:
For fixed-salary employees, overtime is any actual worked time above:

1. 8.4 hours per day
    or
2. 42 hours per week

The overtime hours must be calculated from Ist-Stunden.

Cost calculation:
Overtime cost = overtime hours × employee personal hourly cost rate.

Use the employee’s existing calculated hourly cost rate if available.
Do not invent rates.
If no hourly rate is available, show the overtime hours but mark the cost as unavailable.

Reporting:
In the personnel cost analysis, visually separate:

* regular personnel costs
* overtime additional costs from fixed-salary employees
* total including overtime
* total excluding overtime

Add a toggle button:
“Überstundenkosten einbeziehen”

When enabled:
Include fixed-salary overtime costs in total personnel costs and personnel cost ratio.

When disabled:
Show overtime costs separately but exclude them from totals and ratios.

Display:
For each selected period, show:

* fixed-salary overtime hours
* fixed-salary overtime cost
* affected employees
* daily breakdown
* weekly breakdown

Rules:

* Daily overtime threshold: actual hours above 8.4 per employee per day.
* Weekly overtime threshold: actual hours above 42 per employee per week.
* Avoid double counting.
* If daily overtime already contributes to weekly overtime, calculate the total overtime as the greater of daily-threshold overtime and weekly-threshold overtime for that employee/week, not both added together.
* Only apply this to fixed-salary employees.
* Hourly employees are already costed by actual hours and must not be counted again as overtime Zusatzkosten.

Requirements:

* Respect department filters.
* Respect role permissions.
* Admin can see cost amounts.
* Restricted managers should only see allowed aggregate values according to the existing wage visibility rules.
* Do not expose individual hourly rates to managers.
* Add tests for:
    * employee works 9.4 hours in one day
    * employee works 43 hours in one week
    * daily and weekly overtime overlap without double counting
    * hourly employee is excluded
    * missing hourly cost rate
    * toggle includes overtime in total
    * toggle excludes overtime from total but still displays it separately
    * department filtering
    * fixed employee with no overtime

No database migration unless absolutely necessary.
Do not change import logic.
Use existing Ist-Stunden and employee cost data as source of truth.
