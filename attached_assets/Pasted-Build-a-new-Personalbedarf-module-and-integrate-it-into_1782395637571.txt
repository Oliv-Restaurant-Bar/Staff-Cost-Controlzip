Build a new Personalbedarf module and integrate it into Dienstplanung and Personalkosten.

Goal:
Create a structured staffing demand system that defines the required positions, shifts and personnel units per department and day. This demand must be used to validate the Dienstplanung before and during planning.

Core concepts:

1. Positions and skills
    Add position capability tracking to employees.

Each employee can cover one or more positions.

Examples:
Küche Chef
Küche Warm
Küche Kalt
Pizza
Abwasch
Service
Chef de Service
Bar
Buffet
Springer
Reinigung

In Personalstamm, add a section:
“Kann folgende Positionen abdecken”

Use checkboxes or tags.

2. Staffing demand templates
    Create a new module:
    Personalbedarf

This module defines the required staffing per department, weekday and season.

Fields:
Department
Position
Weekday
Required shift time
Required personnel units
Priority
Active from
Active until
Season or period
Notes

Example:
Küche, Warm, Friday, 10:00 to 14:00 and 17:00 to 23:00, 2 units
Service, Abend, Saturday, 17:00 to 23:30, 4 units

3. Seasonal demand
    The demand must be flexible by period.

Examples:
Normalbetrieb
November
Dezember
Sommer
Ferienzeit
Eventbetrieb

For November and December, allow higher required personnel units and additional positions.

The active demand template should be selected automatically by date.

4. Dienstplanung validation
    When creating or editing the Dienstplan, compare planned employees against the Personalbedarf.

For each day show:
Required positions
Planned positions
Missing positions
Overstaffed positions
Wrongly assigned employees

Example:
Monday Service:
Required: 4 Service units
Planned: 3 Service units
Missing: 1 Service

5. Employee availability and absences
    If an employee is on holiday, sick, accident or absent, they must not count as covering a required position.

Example:
A Service employee is planned but has Ferien.
The system must show:
Service position not covered.

6. Workload and legal planning logic
    Add planning warnings:

100 percent employee should not be planned more than 5 days per week.
Workload based weekly target:
100 percent = 42 h
80 percent = 33.6 h
60 percent = 25.2 h
50 percent = 21 h

Warnings:
Too many planned days
Too many planned hours
Employee planned while absent
Employee planned for a position they cannot cover
Required position not covered
Department demand not fulfilled

7. Planning dashboard
    In Dienstplanung, add a demand coverage panel.

Show per week:
Coverage percentage
Missing shifts
Missing personnel units
Overstaffed units
Projected hours
Projected cost
Budget difference

Use traffic lights:
Green = fully covered and within budget
Orange = missing coverage or slight budget issue
Red = major understaffing or budget exceeded

8. Personal cost integration
    Connect Personalbedarf with Personalkosten.

Show:
Soll hours based on demand
Planned hours
Difference
Soll cost
Planned cost
Difference
Projected PKQ

This must help analyze costs while planning, not only after the month is finished.

9. Flexible staff demand
    Show where flexible staff is needed.

For each week:
Department
Position
Missing units
Missing days
Suggested flexible coverage

Example:
Service needs 2 flexible evening shifts in KW 48.
Küche needs 1 Pizza replacement on Saturday.

10. Replacement suggestions
    If a planned employee is absent or unavailable, suggest replacements based on:
    Position capability
    Department
    Available days
    Remaining weekly workload
    Overtime risk
    Cost impact
11. Do not break existing Dienstplanung
    Keep current scheduling functions working.
    Add the new demand validation as an additional layer.
