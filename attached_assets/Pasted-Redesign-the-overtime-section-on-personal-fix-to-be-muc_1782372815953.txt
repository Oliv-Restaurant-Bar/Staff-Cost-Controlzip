Redesign the overtime section on /personal-fix to be much more compact, manager-friendly and drill-down based.

Goal:
The current weekly overtime table is too wide and difficult to read. Users should first get a compact overview per employee and only see weekly/day details when they click.

New layout:

LEVEL 1 (default view)

Show only one row per employee.

Columns:

Mitarbeiter
Abteilung
Pensum
Soll Monat
Ist Monat
Überstunden Monat
Überstundenkosten
Zusatzkosten
Total Zusatzkosten
Status (Überstunden aktiviert/deaktiviert)

Sort employees by highest overtime first.

Do NOT show every week immediately.

------------------------------------------------

LEVEL 2 (expand employee)

When the user clicks the employee row (or chevron):

Expand directly underneath.

Show a compact weekly table:

KW
Zeitraum
Soll
Ist
Überstunden
Kosten

Example:

KW23    01.06–07.06     42.0     45.2     +3.2     CHF 120
KW24    08.06–14.06     42.0     39.8      0.0     CHF 0
KW25    15.06–21.06     42.0     48.1     +6.1     CHF 230

------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------

UI

Use accordion behaviour:

Employee
    -> Week
        -> Day

Never show all details at once.

Remember expanded rows while staying on the page.

Manual Zusatzkosten

Do NOT mix them into the overtime rows.

Instead:

Inside the expanded employee section show

------------------------------------------------

Performance

Only load/render week rows when an employee is expanded.

Only load/render day rows when a week is expanded.
